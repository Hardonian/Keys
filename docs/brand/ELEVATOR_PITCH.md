## 30-Second Pitch

**Standard Version:**
```
KEYS provides structured assets—prompts, notebooks, runbooks—that complement tools you already use: Cursor, Jupyter, GitHub, Stripe, and more. 
Each asset is reviewed, documented, and designed for adaptation to your context. 
We don't replace tools—we provide starting points that your team evaluates and adapts.
```

**Key Points:**
- KEYS = structured assets, not tools
- Designed for adaptation and review
- Human oversight required at every stage
- Works with tools you own

## 60-Second Pitch

**Standard Version:**
```
KEYS addresses a common challenge: teams have powerful tools but need structured starting points rather than building from scratch.

We provide a library of reviewed assets—prompt patterns, analysis notebooks, operational runbooks—each with documented assumptions and limitations. 
Every asset requires your team's judgment before use.

KEYS works with tools you already own. We don't replace them—we provide starting points that accelerate evaluation while maintaining your control.
```

**Key Points:**
- Problem: Tools are powerful, patterns take time to develop
- Solution: Library of reviewed, documented assets
- Value: Accelerates evaluation, maintains organizational control
- Differentiation: Human oversight required, no autonomy claims

## Language Guidelines

### Use
- "Structured assets for tools you own"
- "Reviewed and documented"
- "Designed for adaptation"
- "Human oversight required"
- "Clear assumptions and limitations"
- "Your team evaluates and decides"

### Avoid
- "AI-powered" or "AI magic"
- "Revolutionary" or "game-changing"
- "Replaces [Tool]" or "automates [X]"
- "Auto-pilot" or "automatic"
- "Best" or "perfect"
- Guaranteed outcomes or ROI claims
- Unverified numeric savings

## Delivery Notes

### For Brief Introductions
- Lead with structure: "KEYS provides reviewed assets for common workflows"
- Emphasize control: "Your team evaluates and adapts"
- Differentiate clearly: "We complement tools—we don't replace judgment"

### For Extended Conversations
- Start with the problem: teams spend time building from scratch
- Explain the solution: library of reviewed, documented assets
- Clarify the model: starting points, not autonomous systems
- Emphasize governance: human oversight, auditability, organizational control

## Common Questions

**Q: "How is this different from ChatGPT or Cursor?"**  
A: "ChatGPT and Cursor generate outputs. KEYS provides reviewed starting points—documented patterns with assumptions and limitations. We provide structure that requires your team's judgment."

**Q: "Why not build it ourselves?"**  
A: "You absolutely can. KEYS may accelerate your evaluation by providing reviewed patterns. Whether it makes sense depends on your specific context and requirements."

**Q: "What if we don't use those tools?"**  
A: "KEYS works with common tools. If you use different tools, our patterns may still be adaptable—each asset is designed for modification to your environment."

**Q: "Do you guarantee outcomes?"**  
A: "No. Every asset is a starting point that requires your team's review and validation. We provide documentation and structure—we don't make claims about results."
