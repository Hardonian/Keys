# Governance

Keys is curated to keep artifacts practical, safe, and adaptable. Governance focuses on transparency, review discipline, and organizational control.

## Curation philosophy

- **Start with reality.** Artifacts reflect how teams actually work, including constraints and tradeoffs. No hidden assumptions.
- **Prefer clarity over completeness.** A shorter, well-scoped artifact is better than a sprawling template.
- **Design for adaptation.** Every artifact is easy to fork and modify. No hidden dependencies or lock-in.

## Review process

All artifacts go through documented review before publication:

1. **Assumption verification.** Maintainers verify documented assumptions, inputs, and expected outputs.
2. **Risk documentation.** Safety, security, and privacy implications are explicitly documented.
3. **Scope tagging.** Artifacts are tagged by type, scope, and change history for traceability.

## Human oversight requirements

- No artifacts are published with autonomous decision-making capability
- All artifacts require human review before application
- Organizations maintain full control over adaptations and customizations
- Administrative changes require human approval gates

## Artifact lifecycle

**Publication:** Artifacts are published with complete documentation including:
- Assumptions and preconditions
- Known limitations and edge cases
- Recommended validation steps

**Deprecation:** When an artifact becomes outdated:
- Marked as deprecated with documented reason
- Replacement link provided where available
- Original retained for auditability

**Replacement:** Updated artifacts include:
- Migration guidance from previous versions
- Breaking changes clearly documented
- Deprecation timeline where applicable

## Organizational control

Your organization retains:
- Full ownership of adapted and forked versions
- Control over which artifacts to adopt
- Authority over validation and review processes
- Portability—no lock-in to Keys infrastructure

## Stewardship model

Governance is transparent and community-facing. Major changes are documented in the repository and open to review by any stakeholder.
