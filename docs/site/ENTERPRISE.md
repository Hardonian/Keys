# Enterprise

Keys is open source and can be used directly by any team. Enterprise is an optional, managed distribution for organizations that need centralized access, access controls, and support.

## What enterprise provides

- Managed distribution of the open source library
- Access controls and workspace provisioning
- Support for procurement, compliance review, and internal onboarding
- SLAs for issue response and remediation

## What enterprise does not change

- The open source artifacts remain the source of truth
- Teams still own adaptation and judgment for their context
- There are no guarantees of outcomes—artifacts are starting points
- Your organization retains full ownership of all artifacts and data

## Engagement boundaries

- Enterprise engagement is scoped to managed distribution, not custom development
- Support covers the managed infrastructure and library access
- Custom requirements are addressed through the standard open source contribution process
- No black-box autonomy—all processing logic is visible and auditable

## Security and governance

- Infrastructure operates with least-privilege access principles
- All actions are logged and available for audit review
- Human approval gates are required for any administrative changes
- Failure modes are documented and escalation paths are defined

## Data and artifact ownership

- All artifacts and data remain with your organization
- No lock-in—standard formats ensure portability
- Export capabilities are available at any time
- Termination clauses provide complete data handoff

If your organization needs a managed distribution, contact the maintainers to discuss scope and support requirements.
